FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

In your own Google Sheets. The add-on only remembers your settings and the list of connected sheets – it does not store your Search Console data.

What do I need to get started?

A Google account with verified access to the website in Google Search Console, and a spreadsheet you can edit.

Which keywords are tracked?

Only the ones you choose. Paste your priority keywords into column A of the report sheet – for example the keywords you agreed on with a client – and the add-on fills in their data every month. You can add or remove keywords anytime.

Is this a rank tracker?

It reports the average position, impressions and top landing page from your own Search Console data – the numbers Google itself measures for your site. It does not scrape search results, so there are no per-keyword credits to buy.

Why 50,000 rows when Search Console only exports 1,000?

The Search Console interface limits exports to 1,000 rows. The add-on reads the same data through the official Search Console API, which allows much larger exports – up to 50,000 rows on the Pro plan.

When is the monthly report updated?

Search Console finalises data with a delay of a few days, so the report for the previous month is collected at the start of each month once the data is complete.

What counts as cannibalization?

A query where two or more of your URLs receive a meaningful share of impressions. The report shows each URL's share and average position, so you can decide which page should own the query.

Does it work with multilingual sites in one GSC property?

Yes. You can include only URLs without a language folder, exclude specific folders such as /de/ or /fr/, or include only selected ones.

How does the free trial work?

You get full access to Pro for 7 days. Cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged.

How do I pay and cancel?

By card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through Stripe. You can cancel anytime from your account; you keep access until the end of the paid period.

Is this a Google product?

No. It's an independent add-on that uses the official Google Search Console API with your permission. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
